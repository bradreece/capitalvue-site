Cookies, website analytics and advertising

When you visit our website we, and our service providers, use cookies and similar technologies (such as pixels, tags and local storage) to make the site work, understand how it is used, and measure and improve our marketing. These technologies may collect information including your device and browser type, IP address, approximate location, the pages you view, the links and buttons you interact with, the website or ad that referred you, and unique cookie or advertising identifiers.

We use the following third-party tools, deployed through Google Tag Manager:

  • Google Analytics and Google Ads (Google LLC) — to measure website traffic and performance, understand how visitors use the site, and measure the effectiveness of our advertising, including conversion tracking and remarketing.
  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms, Inc.) — to measure the performance of our advertising on Facebook and Instagram, and to show relevant ads to people who have visited our website.
  • HubSpot (HubSpot, Inc.) — to operate our contact and enquiry forms, manage our customer relationships and communications, and understand how visitors engage with our content. Our HubSpot account data is hosted in Australia.

We use this information to operate and improve the website, respond to your enquiries, measure which marketing is working, and deliver more relevant advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of interest-based advertising directly with the providers: Google (through Google Ads Settings and the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on) and Meta (through your Facebook and Instagram ad preferences). Each of these providers handles data under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review.
